--- the FAO Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) Sourcebook ---

This booklet provides examples of climate-smart
systems by showcasing some FAO success stories in
various countries. The cases have been selected from
the FAO Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) Sourcebook
launched in 2013 to show the diversity of potential
options across diﬀerent regions and agricultural systems
also covering subjects such as biodiversity and gender.
