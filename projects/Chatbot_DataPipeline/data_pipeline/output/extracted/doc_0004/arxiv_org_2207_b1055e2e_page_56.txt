Explanatory Variables:

The adoption of SAPs has been governed by various
factors. Socioeconomic, demographic, farm attributes and
other related variables were used to predict the relation
with adoption of sustainable agricultural practices among
Kentucky farmers. Ten categorical variables—Crops,
Vegetables, Livestock, Tobacco Buyout Program, Diverse,
Inadequate Knowledge, Irrigation, Off Farm Work, Solo
Proprietorship, Income from Agri-tourism—and four
continuous variables—Land Operated, Age, Education Level,
Income from Farming—were used in the analysis. The
description of variables is in Table 4. Descriptive
statistics of the variables are presented in the Appendix
C, Table 13.
