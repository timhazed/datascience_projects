Sustainable Agricultural Practices:
Sustainable agricultural practices have identified by
various names such as Low External Input Sustainable
Agriculture (LEISA) (Filho et al. 1999; Reijntjes et al.,
1992), and Best Management Practices (BMPs) (Baumgart-Getz,
Prokopy, & Floress, 2012). The fundamental characteristics
of the concepts include use of local resources optimally
(Coze & Hedrich, 2007; Mesner & Paige, 2011), economically
feasible, ecologically sound, culturally adopted, and
socially just (Filho et al., 1999; Reijntjes et al., 1992),
