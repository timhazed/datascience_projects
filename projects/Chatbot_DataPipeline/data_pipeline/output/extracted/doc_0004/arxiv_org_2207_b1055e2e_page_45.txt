Farmers’ Characteristics and Farm Attributes:

(GCFI) are considered as small farms (Hoppe & MacDonald,
2016). Among 230 respondents, 77.90% of farmers have annual
income less than $250,000 with 41.30% of farmers below
$10,000 per year. Only 3.30% of famers have annual income
above $250,000 (Appendix C, Table 10). More than three-
fourth farmers with income below $250,000 shows that the
survey results are more inclined towards predicting the
behavior of small farmers. Also, the survey was dominated
