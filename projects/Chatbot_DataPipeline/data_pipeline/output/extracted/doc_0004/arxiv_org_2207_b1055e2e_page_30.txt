Identifying the role of various socioeconomic,
biological, demographic, farm and other attributes on the
adoption decision has become more important recently due to
changing demographics and regulations. As said by Knowler
and Bradshaw (2007), even a single variable cannot explain
the phenomenon universally. The effect of same variable
may differ based on the location as well the types of
society and farmers. This calls for further research on
understating the site specific effects of variables and
their impacts on farmers’ decision making process.
