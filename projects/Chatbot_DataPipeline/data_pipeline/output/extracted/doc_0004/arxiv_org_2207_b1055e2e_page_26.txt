Coughenour (2003) found that the spread of no-tillage
in Kentucky is due to the interaction and networking among
farmers, cropland and agriculture advisors, company
representatives and agriculture scientists. The networking
among farmers and agencies have large influence in farmer’s
decision to adopt the practices (Carlisle, 2016;
Coughenour, 2003). However, networking could play a role in
both, negative and positive directions: networking with
organic fertilizer dealers negatively influence the
adoption of cover crops in organic farms in several states
in the US (Gabrielyan, Chintawar, & Westra, 2010). Same
variables, however, could not explain the adoption process
consistently throughout the world (Knowler and Bradshaw,
2007).
