Farmers’ pervious participation in the conservation
program has positive influence in the adoption of
additional long-term conservation practices (Featherstone
and Goodwin, 1993; Bergtold et al., 2012; Lichtenberg,
2004; Singer, Nusser, & Alf, 2007; Upadhyay et al., 2003;
Wilson, Howard, & Burnett, 2014). The chains of practices
created following the previous one (also called a “foot in
the door” model) could lead to the complete transformation
of farming systems (Wilson et al., 2014). Farmers, however,
in Missouri are discouraged to transport manure due to its
odor, undigested weed seeds and economic issues (Battle &
Krueger, 2005).
