Data Analysis:
This thesis has three objectives which are discussed
below separately:

Objective I: Farmer’s Perceptions about Farm and Farming
Practice Sustainability:
Farmers’ perception of farm sustainability was explored using
the rating scale data. Farmers were requested to rate their
perception about their farm meeting or implementing the farm
level sustainable agriculture objectives viz. Local Ecosystem
Sustainability, Social Sustainability, Social Acceptability,
and Food System Sustainability in five rating scale—1 being
“Not Sustainable” to 5 being ‘Very Sustainable’. General
frequency count was used and interpreted for this objective.
