Poisson regression is a GLiM with Poisson distribution
error structure and the natural log (ln) link function. The
predicted score in the Poisson regression is not itself a
count, but the natural logarithm of the count. Hence,
Poisson regression is “Linear in the Logarithm”. Indeed,
all form of GLiM has same property—there is a form of the
regression equation that is linear (Coxe et al., 2009).
