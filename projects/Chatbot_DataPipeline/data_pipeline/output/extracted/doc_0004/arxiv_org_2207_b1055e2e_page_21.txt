The SAPs seek to optimize management, reducing
external inputs, as well as use local farm resources,
however, it does not exclude external inputs but
incorporate as complementary to the local resources meeting
five criteria above (Fig. 1.) (Reijntjes et al., 1992).
They are designed to produce least possible adverse impacts
on the environment, human, animal or plant heath (Coxe &
Hedrich, 2007; Mesner & Paige, 2011) as well as protect the
biological sources (Lashgarara, 2011).
