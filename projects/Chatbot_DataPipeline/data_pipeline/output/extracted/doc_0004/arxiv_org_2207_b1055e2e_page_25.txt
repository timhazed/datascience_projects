Baumgart-Getz et al. (2012) identified capital as the
best financial predictor of adoption behavior. Carlisle
(2016) further mentioned financial barriers as an important
decision making factor for the adoption of soil health
related practices. However, immediate financial benefits
are less important compared to long-term soil health, if
practices have been already adopted. US farmers adopted
improved seed more rapidly compared to conservation tillage
practice (Lambert, Sullivan, Claassen, Foreman, 2006) as
conservation tillage require different set of tools which
is wise to adopt only when old technologies are ready to be
replaced (Krause and Black, 1995).
