Objectives and Hypothesis
Objectives:
The specific objectives were:
1) to explore Farmer’s Perceptions about Farm and
Farming Practice Sustainability
2) to identify predictors of SAPs adoption using farm
attributes, farmers’ attitudes and behaviors,
socioeconomic and demographic factors, and
knowledge.
3) to evaluate adoption barriers of SAPs among Kentucky
Farmers.

Hypothesis:
The adoption of SAPs among Kentucky farmers have
significant relationship with their farm attributes,
farmers’ attitudes, socioeconomic, and demographic factors.
