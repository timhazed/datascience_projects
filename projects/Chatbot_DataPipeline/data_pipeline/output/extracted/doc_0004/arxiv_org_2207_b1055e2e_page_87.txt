--- Improved Water Management: ---

82

Improved Water Management: Use of improved irrigation
facilities to reduce losses of irrigation water (Kornegay et
al., 2010).
Increase Biodiversity: Increase diversity by adding various
plants and animals in the farm (Kornegay et al., 2010).
Integrated Pest Management: A pest management strategy
using a systematic approach in which pest population are
monitored to determine if and when control methods are
required. It uses biological, chemical, physical, cultural
production cost and protect the environment (Kornegay et
al., 2010).
Land Reforming (to reduce erosion): Forming terrace, reducing
slope, and other slope stabilizing technologies to reduce
surface run off of water and top soil.
Local or Native Crops: Locally available crops or local
varieties (Kornegay et al., 2010).
