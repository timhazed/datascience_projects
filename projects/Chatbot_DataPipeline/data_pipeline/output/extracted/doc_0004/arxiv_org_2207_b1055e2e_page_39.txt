The probability of a specific count also depends on
the variance (σ2) of the number of counts. However, the
poison distribution has only one parameter µ i.e. both the
conditional mean and the conditional variance are equal to
µ, the condition is called as equi-dispersion (Coxe et al.,
2009; Avei, Alturk, & Soylu, 2015). This property of
Poisson distribution, i.e. the variance increases with the
increase in mean and vice versa, is helpful in modeling
count outcome (Coxe et al., 2009).
