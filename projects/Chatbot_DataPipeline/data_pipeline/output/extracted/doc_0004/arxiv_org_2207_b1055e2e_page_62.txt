Farmers cultivating row crops (Crops) variable is
significant (p = 0.000) and has a positive relationship
with the adoption of SAPs in Kentucky. The change in the
expected log count of SAPs adoption would be expected to
increase by 1.067 if farmers have row crops in their farm.
Several researchers have found that farmers diversify their
farms by adding high value crops, short season crops and
cover crops (Lambert et al., 2006; Lichtenberg 2004; Singer
et al., 2007; Snapp et al., 2005). Cover crops are helpful
in contributing weed control, reduce use of chemicals, soil
health, fertility, and improved crop performance thus
sustaining the production (Teasdale, 2013).
