Abstract
The Green Care and Social Agriculture (GC-SF) exist in Portugal, and its concept is emerging. The
need to disseminate the GC-SF concept and the creation of an arena for sharing experiences and
to demonstrate the need for a political and legal framework that allows the interaction between
the sectors of Agriculture, Health, Welfare and Justice, led ESAC and APCC to the organization of
the First Meeting of Social Agriculture at Coimbra, Portugal in April 15, 2011. This meeting was an
opportunity to share the international and national experiences among participants, and provide a
national feedback about GC-SF. For this reason during the conference it was requested to the
participants to answer a small questionnaire. The feedback form include some of the following
questions: what are the benefits, the opportunities and the barriers of green care in Portugal?
