There are already proven soil management practices that can help farmers adapt to the likely adverse effects of increasing weather variability and climate change. These practices also often reduce GHG emissions from agriculture5 and build resilience in farming systems (see Annex A.4.1). Widespread adoption of these prac- tices has the potential to make major contributions to the achievement of national food security and develop- ment goals. There is a need to assess and provide incentives for the adoption of systems with the greatest production, mitigation and adaptation potential (win-win-win).
