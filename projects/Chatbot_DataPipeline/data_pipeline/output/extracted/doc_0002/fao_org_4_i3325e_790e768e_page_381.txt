To formulate CSA strategies and interventions reliable information, including good quality data, documented vulnerabilities and accurate evidence is required. Frequently, these data are lacking or are only available for some sub-sectors. In Africa, for example, while estimates of food produced for subsistence are generally mon- etized and reflected in GDP estimates, this is often not the case for timber and non-timber forest products.
