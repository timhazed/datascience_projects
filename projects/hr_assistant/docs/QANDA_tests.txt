Based on **The Nestlé Human Resources Policy (September 2012)**, here are 10 questions and answers derived from the document:

### **Policy Overview and Responsibilities**

1. **What is the core spirit of the Nestlé Human Resources Policy?**
* The spirit of the policy is summarized by the belief that Nestlé puts people at the center of everything they do.




2. **Who holds the prime responsibility for sustaining a committed work environment?**
* Line managers have the primary responsibility for building and sustaining an environment where employees feel a personal commitment to their work.




3. **What are the three dedicated areas of the Human Resources structure?**
* The HR structure is based on **Centres of Expertise** (specialized services), **Business Partners** (deploying HR strategies), and **Employee Services** (transactional activities).





### **Employment and Recruitment**

4. **What criteria are considered when hiring a new employee at Nestlé?**
* Nestlé hires based on relevant skills, experience, professional skills for long-term relationships, and adherence to Nestlé principles.




5. **What factors are strictly excluded from consideration during the recruitment process?**
* No consideration is given to a candidate's origin, nationality, religion, race, gender, disability, sexual orientation, or age.




6. **Does Nestlé's commitment to good working conditions apply to outside contractors?**
* Yes; Nestlé insists that service providers take steps to ensure adequate working conditions are available to people working under contractual obligations.





### **Employee Growth and Relations**

7. **What elements define "Total Rewards" at Nestlé?**
* Total Rewards include Fixed Pay, Variable Pay, Benefits, Personal Growth and Development, and Work Life Environment.




8. **What is the primary source of learning for Nestlé employees?**
* Experience and on-the-job training are considered the primary sources of learning.




9. **What tools are used to provide feedback on performance and career aspirations?**
* Employees receive feedback through the Performance Evaluation process (PE), the Progress and Development Guide (PDG), and $360^{\circ}$ assessments.




10. **How does Nestlé approach the right to collective bargaining?**
* Nestlé upholds the freedom of association and the effective recognition of the right to collective bargaining for its employees.
