## Red Flag Triage (Safety Matrix) - Hard-Stops

| Category | Keywords / Symptoms (Triage) | Action |
|----------|------------------------------|--------|
| Neurological | Numbness, Tingling, "Electrical zap," "Radiating," Shooting pain, Loss of motor control. | REJECT: Refer to ER/Neurologist. |
| Cardiovascular | Chest pain/fluttering, Palpitations, Dizziness at rest, Syncope, Extreme dyspnea. | REJECT: Refer to Cardiologist. |
